What we are looking for

THE WORK COMES FIRST.

Live action can meet generation. Photography can become motion. Traditional illustration can mix with models, VFX, 3D and code. There is no minimum percentage of AI. We care about the idea, the craft and what the technology made possible.
FILM

Narrative, documentary, experimental, live action and hybrid cinema.

MOTION

Animation, motion design, music visuals, VFX and generative moving image.

VISUAL ART

Photography, illustration, static digital art, generative art and mixed media.

OPEN

Installation, interactive, spatial, audiovisual and work that doesn't fit an existing category.
