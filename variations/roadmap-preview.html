What we are looking for

THE WORK COMES FIRST.

Live action can meet generation. Photography can become motion. Traditional illustration can mix with models, VFX, 3D and code. There is no minimum percentage of AI. We care about the idea, the craft and what the technology made possible.

FILM

Narrative, documentary, experimental, live action and hybrid cinema.

MOTION

Animation, motion design, music visuals, VFX and generative moving image.

VISUAL ART

Photography, illustration, static digital art, generative art and mixed media.

OPEN

Installation, interactive, spatial, audiovisual and work that doesn’t fit an existing category.

FAQs

BEFORE
YOU SUBMIT.

Can I share my work now?

Yes. We’d love to see what you’re making. Send a link to your work and a short introduction to hello@variantfest.com. The official open call is still being finalized, so this is an early introduction rather than a formal submission. Join the list and we’ll let you know when and how to submit.

What work can I submit?

We’re planning categories for film, motion, visual art, and experimental work made with AI. Final categories and eligibility rules will be published when submissions open.

What are the deadlines and fees?

Submissions are free. We’ll announce deadlines when the open call opens. Join the list to hear when it’s time to submit.

Where and when is the festival?

Late February 2027 in Los Angeles is the planned window. Exact dates, venue, and schedule are not yet confirmed.
